15 · Initialization & Normalization

Some problems start before the first gradient step

Weight initialization

  • Xavier / Glorot — balanced variance for sigmoid/tanh activations
  • He initialization — corrected scale for ReLU networks
  • Bad initialization causes vanishing or exploding gradients from the very first forward pass

Normalization layers

  • BatchNorm — normalizes activations across the minibatch at each layer
  • LayerNorm — normalizes across features; standard in transformers
  • Both smooth the loss landscape and allow larger, more stable learning rates
Good initialization and normalization do not replace a well-tuned optimizer — but they can make the optimizer's job dramatically easier from step one.
