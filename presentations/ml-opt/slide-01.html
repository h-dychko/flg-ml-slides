Optimization for ML

Optimization for
Machine Learning

How models actually learn: from gradient descent basics to SGD, AdaGrad, RMSProp, Adam, and the practical tricks that make training work.

What this talk covers

  • Why optimization matters
  • Loss landscapes and gradients
  • Optimizer evolution: GD → SGD → Adam
  • Learning rates, schedules, and debugging
  • Optimization vs generalization

Main message

In practice, optimization is not just about finding a minimum. It is about finding a good solution fast, stably, and in a way that still generalizes to unseen data.
