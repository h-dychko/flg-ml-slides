11 · The Learning Rate

Learning rate is often the most important hyperparameter

Too high

  • Loss oscillates or diverges
  • Updates overshoot the minimum
  • Can produce NaN in gradients

Too low

  • Training is painfully slow
  • Can get trapped in flat regions
  • Wastes compute budget

Well-tuned

  • Fast, stable loss decrease
  • Smooth gradient updates
  • Makes the best use of optimizer strengths
Before switching optimizers or changing architecture — tune the learning rate first. It is the single highest-leverage knob in training.
