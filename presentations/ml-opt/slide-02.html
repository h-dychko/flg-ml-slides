1 · Motivation

Training = minimizing a loss function

The model has parameters θ. The loss tells us how bad current predictions are. Optimization updates parameters to reduce that loss.

θ ← θ − η ∇θL(θ)
Optimization answers one concrete question: how do we move parameters so the loss decreases?

Why it matters

  • Same architecture can train very differently with another optimizer
  • Learning rate often matters more than model tweaks
  • Optimization affects speed, stability, and final test quality
