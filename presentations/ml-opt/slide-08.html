7 · Adaptive Methods

AdaGrad

Algorithm

Accumulate the sum of squared gradients and scale each parameter's update by its own gradient history. Frequent parameters get smaller steps; rare ones keep larger steps.

rt = rt−1 + gt2
θt+1 = θt − η · gt / (√rt + ε)

Pros & cons

Pros
✓Per-parameter learning rates — no manual tuning per feature
✓Works well with sparse gradients (NLP, embeddings)
✓Adaptive step size increases effective learning for rare features
Cons
✗rt grows forever — learning rate shrinks to near zero
✗Training often stalls before convergence on deep nets
