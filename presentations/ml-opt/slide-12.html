10 · Optimizer Evolution

Each optimizer fixes a problem with the previous one

Step 1

Gradient Descent

Foundational. Full-batch gradient, stable but slow and impractical at scale.

stable
Step 2

SGD

Minibatch updates — much faster per step. Noise is a feature, not just a bug.

scalable
Step 3

Momentum

Smooths gradient direction. Dampens oscillation, accelerates useful progress.

smoother
Step 4

AdaGrad / RMSProp

Per-parameter learning rates. RMSProp fixes AdaGrad's vanishing LR problem.

adaptive
Step 5

Adam

Momentum + adaptive scaling + bias correction. The standard default today.

default today
