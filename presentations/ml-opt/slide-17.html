14 · Regularization

Regularization shapes the optimization path

⚖️

L2 / Weight decay

Penalizes large weights. Shrinks parameters toward zero each step, which changes the effective loss surface that the optimizer navigates.

Ltotal = L + λ ‖θ‖2
🎲

Dropout

Randomly zeros activations during training. Injects noise into the optimization trajectory, which can lead to more robust minima.

🛑

Early stopping

Halt training when validation loss stops improving. The optimizer never reaches the training minimum — and that is intentional.
