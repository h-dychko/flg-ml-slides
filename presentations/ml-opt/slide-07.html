Nesterov Accelerated Gradient

Key idea

Instead of computing the gradient at the current position, compute it at the anticipated next position — where momentum would carry us.

θ̃t = θt − η β vt−1  // look-ahead
gt = ∇θ L(θ̃t)  // gradient at look-ahead
vt = β vt−1 + (1−β) gt
θt+1 = θt − η vt

Pros & cons

Pros
✓Reacts to upcoming gradient — can brake before overshooting
✓Often converges faster than classical momentum
✓Provably optimal convergence rate for convex problems
Cons
✗Slightly more complex to implement correctly
✗Gains over standard momentum are smaller in practice on deep nets
Think of Nesterov as momentum with a look-ahead gradient: instead of correcting after you overshoot, you correct before.
