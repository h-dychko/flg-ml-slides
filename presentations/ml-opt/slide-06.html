5 · Momentum

SGD with Momentum

Algorithm

Accumulate an exponentially weighted average of past gradients. Update parameters using this velocity, not the raw gradient.

vt = β vt−1 + (1−β) gt
θt+1 = θt − η vt

Pros & cons

Pros
✓Dampens oscillations in steep directions
✓Accelerates progress in consistent directions
✓Can reach better final accuracy than Adam
Cons
✗One extra hyperparameter β to tune (typically 0.9)
✗Still requires careful learning rate selection
Momentum makes optimization behave less like random jitter and more like a ball rolling downhill with inertia.
