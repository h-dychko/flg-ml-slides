Summary & Discussion
GD → SGD → Momentum → RMSProp / Adam

The conceptual arc

  • Gradient gives a direction to move
  • SGD makes training scalable via minibatches
  • Momentum stabilizes and accelerates updates
  • Adaptive methods rescale steps per parameter
  • Learning rate is the most impactful knob

The bigger picture

  • Optimization speed does not equal generalization quality
  • Regularization, init, and normalization all shape training
  • Sharp vs flat minima matter for test performance
  • Debugging training is a core ML engineering skill
